# Comeback OS design

The desktop dashboard takes its geometry from a basketball half court and its information hierarchy from an athlete's session sheet. The current session owns the wide left column. A readiness rail stays adjacent at desktop sizes; the rails stack when the window is snapped. The workout player replaces the session sheet with an oversized interval clock and explicit next-set cue.

Surfaces are #0B111E and #131C2E, with #1034A6 for active controls. Stone Blue sets panel boundaries and recovery states. Warning tints derive from #FF2400 and #E02000. Text uses #F4F6F9 and #94A3B8. Use a condensed athletic face for session headings, a clear sans for controls and JetBrains Mono for measured values.

The supplied color reference establishes a deep-navy session lead with a darker blue left field and a Stone Blue court field on the right. Use solid Scarlet on the primary Start/Resume action and for the stop threshold, safety and countdown signals. Egyptian Blue remains the selection and progress color.

Use court geometry as quiet linework and in movement diagrams. Keep buttons, keyboard focus, and pressed states visible. Preserve normal Tab navigation. Use motion for exercise trajectories and timing, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
